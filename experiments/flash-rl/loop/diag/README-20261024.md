Trigger: milestone 39 — policy u47 vs start policy, same cases: public 113 joint 32 vs 34, sealed 11 vs 12, original12 16 vs 16
(McNemar p≈1 on each). Loop paused after eval-u050 for this diagnosis (user: "해봐").

## 1. How far the policy moved
- LoRA ‖ΔW‖_F summed over all adapted modules (`/tmp/lora_norms.py` on KT, scale α/r = 1):
  init 0.006 · u010 0.71 · u020 1.49 · u030 1.92 · u040 1.98 · u047 2.17 · u050 2.25. ~94% of the norm sits in the rank-2 expert LoRAs.
  Growth is near-linear at lr 1e-4 (u2–u30) and almost flat after the rollbacks (u30→u40 +0.07 at lr 2.5e-5).
- Same tokens re-scored on the serving vLLM (`diag/logprob_shift.py`, 24 rollout turns of u048–u050, 11k completion tokens):
  mean log π_policy − log π_base = **0.011 nat/token** (sample KL estimate on policy-sampled tokens); mean |Δ| 0.094 vs the
  base-vs-base repeat noise floor 0.059. Per token the policy is barely distinguishable from the base.

## 2. Data and learning rate
- One update = **one optimizer step** (all microbatches accumulated). Whole run: 42 released updates, **37 optimizer steps**,
  460 episodes (8–16 per step), ~27M generated tokens. Typical RLVR runs use hundreds of steps × 64–1024 episodes per step.
- Learning rate cut 1e-4 → 5e-5 → 2.5e-5 by two rollbacks, both now look like false alarms:
  - u31 (10-20): "validation10 14 vs best 17" — best-so-far 17 was a single peak (u27); 14 is the usual level (u9–u30: 13–15).
  - u35 (10-21): "validation10 11 vs best 17" — 11 was measured in a new epoch (prompt rev 2 + new rca-mcp) and compared with a
    best from the old epoch. The rollback criterion became start-policy-reference based on 10-21, after both.
  Each rollback also reverted the policy to u27 (adapter-580d72), discarding u28–u34.
- Time per update ≈ 1 h rollout + 2.5–3.5 h training; training (one pass over 12 long trajectories) dominates.

## 3. Reward direction (rollout groups)
- Per-group reward spread 0.2–0.9 (std ~0.25), informative groups are selected by dynamic sampling, so each step has signal.
- Rollout joint rate: u5–u18 31/96 (0.32), u19–u34 60/180 (0.33), u36–u50 79/184 (0.43) — confounded by case selection
  (solved-extra groups from u27, masks/variants), so not evidence of learning by itself.

not accumulate in output space. Per-token abs differences sit at the vLLM batch-nondeterminism floor (~0.08).

## 5. CORRECTION 2026-10-26: the configured learning rate never applied
adapter.pt optimizer param_groups lr = 5e-5 in u005, u020, u030, u040, u050, u055, u062. GeneralUpdate builds AdamW with the
configured lr and then calls load_state_dict(start optimizer), which restores the saved param_group lr; the first update saved
5e-5, so every later update ran at 5e-5: the 10-18 raise to 1e-4, both rollback halvings and the 10-24 "A" restore had no effect.
§2's "lr cut to 2.5e-5" was wrong in effect; the u30→u40 stall came from the rollbacks reverting the policy to u27.
The faster weight movement after 10-24 came from per-group steps (B) alone. Fix (fast18/fast19, diag/patch_lr_fix.py, CPU test
diag/test_lr_fix.py): re-apply the configured lr after load_state_dict; release records effective_learning_rate.
From u64 the configured 1e-4 actually applies (2x the lr every earlier update used).
